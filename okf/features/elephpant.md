# Elephpant

The elephpant is the generic name for the unofficial logo of PHP. It was designed by Vincent Pontier. 

Later, it was transformed into a cute plush toy by Damien Seguy.

The name elePHPant is a neologism, and is characteristic of the PHP community. The extra P in the word elephant makes the name of PHP appear, while turning the word into a tongue-twister.
