# Anonymize

To anonymize is to replace a precise identifier with an unrelated piece of data. For example, ``Mr Elephpant`` may be renamed ``Mr Smith`` or ``b0d7b3834d527db3b2ccd6e4b0f3e0e6``.

Anonymization removes anything that can trace back to the original user. It must also keep relationships with other data, which must also be anonymized. Some values cannot be anonymized, such as booleans.

Anonymization is distinct from fake data, which creates valid-looking data out of nothing.
