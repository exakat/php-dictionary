# Adapters

Adapters are the link between the application and the rest of the universe. They take incoming data and shape them into an internal format, and vice-versa.

Adapters are heavily used in hexagonal architecture. They are also a classic design pattern.

Adapters are related to decoupling code. They bridge two pieces of code that evolve independently.
